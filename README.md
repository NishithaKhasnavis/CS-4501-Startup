Idea 5 - 
A browser extension type app paired with a writing add-on that automatically builds the reference list for your assignments, papers and presentations from the websites you actually used. While you visit online websites/papers/ai models, it records the pages you visit, how long/closely you read them (maybe how far down you scrolled), and any text you copy. When you’re done writing, it compares your document/presentation against those pages to find direct quotes and paraphrased ideas, then adds properly formatted citations for every source you drew from. The reference list can possibly carry an invisible watermark so it can be verified, while the citations are normal and can be chosen from APA/MLA/etc. 
  Purpose is to help students and writers never forget or miss a source. It could also be used by educators to enforce a method to prevent plagiarism, encourage diverse sources and check that the sources used are credible.

Idea 6 - Dropbox add-on: Smart file cleanup and version recovery
A tool that connects to Dropbox and automatically identifies duplicate files, outdated drafts, confusing folder structures, and documents that are difficult to find. It recommends a cleaner organization system and lets users recover previous versions or locate files using natural-language descriptions.
The addition: Instead of just storing and syncing files, Dropbox would actively maintain your workspace and prevent digital clutter from accumulating.

Idea 7 - DoorDash add-on: Group ordering with intelligent cost optimization
A feature that lets groups of friends, coworkers, or students combine orders from nearby restaurants. The system finds compatible orders, optimizes delivery timing, splits fees fairly, and allows each person to pay separately.
For example, five students ordering lunch near campus could combine their orders to reduce delivery fees without having to coordinate payments manually.
The addition: Instead of treating every customer as an individual order, DoorDash could optimize deliveries around groups of people in the same location.
